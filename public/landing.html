How It Works

Up and Running in 3 Steps

No technical knowledge needed. No CSV columns to map. Just connect and go.

1

Install on Both Stores

Install Importzo from the Shopify App Store on your source store and your destination store. It's free — no credit card needed.

2

On the source store, generate a one-time session code. Enter it on the destination store to securely pair the two stores.

3

Select & Start Transfer

Choose which modules to transfer — products, themes, pages, menus, or everything. Hit Start and Importzo handles the rest automatically.
